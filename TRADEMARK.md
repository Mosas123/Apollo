## Forks

Forks and modified builds are welcome under the GPL, but if you distribute one
you must:

- use a different app name and bundle identifier;
- replace the icon and any Apollo or cl0udly branding;
- not imply endorsement by, or affiliation with, the Apollo project;
- keep the GPL-3.0 license, the source code and the copyright notices.

Do not publish a fork as "Apollo", "Apollo Music" or any name/icon confusingly
similar, and do not use the official bundle identifier `com.cl0udly.Apollo`.

## Official builds

Official builds are published only from
[github.com/Mosas123/Apollo](https://github.com/Mosas123/Apollo). Builds from
any other source are unofficial, including paid copies.

## Reporting

If you find a build using the Apollo name, icon or bundle identifier without
permission, or claiming to be official, open an issue in this repository.

This policy does not limit any right granted by GPL-3.0 over the code. It
applies to the project's names, logos and other brand assets.
